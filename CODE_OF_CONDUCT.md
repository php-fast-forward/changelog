# Community code of conduct

This policy applies to participation in the Fast Forward Changelog repository,
including issues, pull requests, reviews and other project-managed spaces.
People of every background and experience level are welcome to participate.

## Expected behavior

Keep discussion respectful and relevant to the project. Explain technical
concerns with evidence, give actionable feedback, and allow others to disagree
or ask questions. Respect each participant's stated boundaries and privacy.

Threats, targeted harassment, discriminatory insults, unwanted sexual remarks,
and publishing someone else's private information without consent are
unacceptable. Do not continue unwanted contact after a participant asks you to
stop. Disagreement about code does not justify attacks on its author.

## Reports and moderation

Send conduct concerns privately to
[github@mentordosnerds.com](mailto:github@mentordosnerds.com). Include links or
examples and explain any immediate safety or privacy concern. Share only the
information needed to assess the report.

Maintainers may request a change in behavior, remove disruptive content, or
restrict participation temporarily or permanently according to the severity and
pattern of conduct. Reports are handled with attention to privacy; information
may be shared when necessary to investigate or address the concern. Someone
involved in a report should not decide the outcome of that report alone.
