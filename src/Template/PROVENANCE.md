# Keep a Changelog template provenance

The built-in headings and English introduction adapt the Keep a Changelog
1.1.0 convention. The Brazilian Portuguese category names follow its official
Portuguese principles. The Portuguese introduction and missing-note messages
are translations authored for this package; they are not presented as an
upstream Portuguese Markdown template.
